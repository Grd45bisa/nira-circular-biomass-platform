# NIRA Design System


# 1. Design Philosophy

## "Natural Premium Circular"

Visual direction:

- Natural
- Warm
- Sustainable
- Modern
- Human centered


Website should feel:

Not:
- Industrial waste management

But:
- Premium eco innovation brand

# 6. Image Direction


Photography:

- Natural lighting
- Human interaction
- Close-up texture


Subject:

- Coconut fiber
- Hands working
- Community
- Green environment


Avoid:

- Generic stock photo
- Industrial factory feeling


---


# 7. Animation


Style:

Slow
Natural
Organic


Examples:

- Fade up
- Parallax leaf movement
- Product transformation animation


Avoid:

- Fast movement
- Excessive effects
