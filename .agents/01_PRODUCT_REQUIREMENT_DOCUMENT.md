# Product Requirement Document

## Product Name

NIRA Website

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Product Vision

Create a digital platform that communicates how coconut waste
transformation creates environmental value, economic opportunity, and
community impact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Product Goals

## Brand Goal

Build NIRA as a recognizable sustainability brand.

## Communication Goal

Help visitors understand: - What NIRA does - Why it matters - How
transformation happens - What impact is created

## Business Goal

Support: - Partnership opportunities - Product awareness - Brand
credibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Main User Journey

User enters website

↓

Understand NIRA story

↓

Learn coconut transformation

↓

Explore products

↓

See impact

↓

Take action

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Website Scope

Included: - Homepage - About - Transformation - Products - Impact -
Journal - Partnership

Excluded for now: - Login - Dashboard - Admin panel - Marketplace -
Payment system

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Homepage Objective

The homepage must communicate:

"NIRA transforms overlooked coconut resources into valuable solutions
for nature and communities."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Required Homepage Sections

## Hero

Purpose: Create first impression.

## Story Behind NIRA

Explain problem and opportunity.

## Coconut Transformation

Show how one coconut creates multiple possibilities.

## Product Showcase

Introduce product categories.

## Impact Section

Show environmental and social value.

## Community Story

Humanize the brand.

## Call To Action

Encourage collaboration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Functional Requirements

System should support: - Text content - Images - Product information -
Impact metrics

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Quality Requirements

The website must be:

Fast: Optimized loading.

Accessible: Clear typography and navigation.

Maintainable: Clean component structure.

Premium: Consistent visual experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Success Indicators

Brand: - Increased awareness - Stronger credibility

Engagement: - Visitor interaction - Product exploration

Business: - Partnership inquiries - Collaboration interest
